# Rubric: Ideation

Evaluate the concept and framing of a piece before or during drafting.

| Criterion | Not Yet | Developing | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Frame / Angle** | Retells a familiar take without a new lens | Has a fresh angle but doesn't fully commit to it | The piece is organized around a lens, metaphor, or conceptual frame only this writer would find |
| **Vulnerability** | The writer's personal connection to the topic is unclear or absent | The writer is present but staying safe — revealing without risk | The writer is exposing something real and the reader feels the stakes of that exposure |
| **Perspective** | Could be written by anyone with the same information | Has a point of view but hedges or qualifies it away | Brings a perspective only this writer could bring — shaped by specific experience and conviction |
| **Timeliness** | No particular reason to read this now | Loosely connected to current moment | Arrives at the right time — the reader needed this piece |
| **Scope** | Too broad to say anything meaningful, or too narrow to matter | Roughly scoped but could be tighter | Right-sized: says one thing well |
