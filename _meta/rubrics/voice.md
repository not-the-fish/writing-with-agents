# Rubric: Voice and Register

Evaluate whether the writer is showing up as the right version of themselves for the context. Consult `_meta/voice_and_style.md` for the writer's tonal range across registers.

| Criterion | Not Yet | Developing | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Register Match** | The tone is mismatched to the audience and purpose | Tone mostly fits but borrows too heavily from another register | The tone fits the context — a newsletter sounds like a newsletter, an essay sounds like an essay — and both sound like the same person |
| **Writer Visibility** | The person behind the writing has disappeared into institutional or generic mode | The writer is present in places but retreats into safe, impersonal language elsewhere | The writer is identifiable throughout — even the professional voice has a human behind it |
| **Vulnerability Calibration** | Too guarded for the register (holding back in a personal essay) or too exposed (oversharing in professional writing) | Vulnerability is present but not well-calibrated — goes too far or not far enough for the context | The right amount for the register — the reader feels the writer's stakes without discomfort or distance |
| **Hedging Check** | Academic caution is dampening claims that should be strong — "it seems" and "I suspect" where the writer knows what they think | Some hedging that doesn't serve the piece, but the writer commits to key claims | Qualifications are deliberate and earned — hedging where genuine uncertainty exists, asserting where the writer has conviction |
