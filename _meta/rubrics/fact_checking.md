# Rubric: Fact Checking

Evaluate the accuracy and sourcing of claims, especially in newsletter and professional writing.

| Criterion | Not Yet | Developing | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Source Quality** | Claims are unsourced or rely on unreliable sources | Some sourcing but inconsistent quality | Claims are backed by credible, current sources |
| **Claim Accuracy** | Contains factual errors or misleading framing | Mostly accurate with some imprecise claims | Every factual claim holds up to scrutiny |
| **Context** | Facts are presented without relevant context | Some context but key nuances are missing | Facts are situated — the reader understands what they mean |
| **Attribution** | Sources are not credited | Informal attribution without enough detail | Sources are clearly credited with enough detail to verify |
