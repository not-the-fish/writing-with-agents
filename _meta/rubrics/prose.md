# Rubric: Prose

Evaluate sentence-level craft and voice.

| Criterion | Not Yet | Developing | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Clarity** | The reader has to re-read to understand | Mostly clear with some muddy patches | Every sentence communicates on first read |
| **Rhythm and Sound** | Sentences are monotonous in length and structure | Some variation but not yet musical | Sentence length, structure, and sound serve the feeling of the piece — rewards being read aloud |
| **Momentum** | The prose stalls or the reader needs breaks to get through dense passages | Some sections pull the reader forward but others lose propulsive force | The prose generates its own forward motion — the reader wants to keep going, even through depth and complexity |
| **Voice Consistency** | Shifts tone without intention | Mostly consistent with a few slips | Sounds like the writer throughout — intentional register shifts (e.g., moving between analytical and vulnerable) are clearly deliberate |
| **Precision** | Relies on vague or generic language | Occasionally sharp, often approximate | Words are chosen, not settled for |
| **Imagery** | Abstract where it could be concrete | Some strong images mixed with flat passages | Sensory details do real work — the reader sees, hears, feels |
