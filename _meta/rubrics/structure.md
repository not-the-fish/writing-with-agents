# Rubric: Structure

Evaluate the organization, pacing, and architecture of a draft.

| Criterion | Not Yet | Developing | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Arc** | No discernible movement from beginning to end | There's a shape but it meanders or stalls | Clear trajectory — the reader is carried somewhere |
| **Governing Frame** | No metaphor, conceit, or conceptual structure organizing the piece | A frame is present but decorative — it doesn't do structural work | A governing metaphor or conceit organizes the piece and deepens or evolves with each return |
| **Pacing** | Sections feel rushed or drag without purpose | Mostly paced well but momentum flags in the back half | The piece maintains force through its full depth — the final third is as propulsive as the first |
| **Transitions** | Jumps between ideas feel abrupt or arbitrary | Transitions exist but feel mechanical | Movement between ideas feels natural and earned |
| **Opening** | Doesn't give the reader a reason to continue | Serviceable but not compelling | Hooks the reader with voice, stakes, or surprise |
| **Closing** | Ends abruptly or trails off | Wraps up but doesn't resonate | Lands — the reader sits with it |

## Notes

<!-- Refine these criteria over time. Not every piece needs a governing frame — some are better served by straightforward structure. But when one is present, evaluate whether it's doing real work. -->
