# Rubric: Memoir Craft

Evaluate craft challenges specific to memoir and creative nonfiction. Use alongside the Prose and Structure rubrics, which cover sentence-level and architectural concerns.

| Criterion | Not Yet | Developing | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Self-Portraiture** | The writer is either absent from the story or presented as blameless | The writer includes unflattering details but frames them defensively or with excessive self-punishment | The writer presents themselves honestly — flaws serve the story, not a performance of guilt or humility |
| **Representing Others** | Other people are props, villains, or cardboard — serving the writer's narrative without their own complexity | Other people are present but unevenly drawn — some feel real, others feel like functions of the plot | Other people are rendered as full humans with their own logic, even when the relationship is painful or the writer disagrees with them |
| **Memory and Truth** | Reconstructed scenes are presented as fact without acknowledging the limits of memory | Some awareness of memory's unreliability but inconsistent in signaling it | The writer is honest about what they remember, what they've reconstructed, and where the gaps are — without undermining the narrative |
| **Emotional Restraint vs. Directness** | The piece tells the reader what to feel or over-explains the emotional significance of events | Emotional moments mostly land but some are over-stated or under-earned | The piece earns its emotional moments — and knows when withholding or letting structure carry the feeling would hit harder than direct statement |
